MNDOT

PRODUCT/GAME DESIGN

PROJECT BRIEF

Develop and design an interactive classroom activity for students in 11-15 age range to learn about the Minnesota Department of Transportation's curriculum on pedestrian safety. The game has to be easily accessible for classrooms and teachers and modular for different resources and time frames.

STRATEGY

A competitive racing style board game, where students answer and collect trading cards to get to their destinations safely. With a celebration of different lifestyles and modes of transportation, students can have fun and compete while learning pedestrian safety.

CHARACTERS AND GRAPHICS

With the established style guide coming from MNDot, the main goal of the games new graphics were to expand the concept to include different lifestyles and modes of transportation. This included ensuring both rural and urban elements were present. This included playgrounds, forests, skyscrapers, and many different characters.

QUESTION CARDS

The question cards were created in mind with easy access for teachers with limited supplies, as well as easy readability for students. They were designed to work both in color and black and white printing based on access to classroom resources, and kept simple to highlight larger text and simple patterns. The backside pattern took inspiration from the MNDot brand styleguide, and the front side to highlight the established characters from the MNDot brand.
