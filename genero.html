GENERO

PROJECT BRIEF

Conceptualize and design a series of products under a parent generic brand. There should be a large variety of different packaging shapes and forms to display brand application.

STRATEGY

Genero, a creativity forward kids craft kit, meant to inspire and celebrate the imagination and creativity of children. Utilizing a strict color system with fantastical illustrations to contrast the more generic copy.

REAL WORLD APPLICATION

Compared to other kids craft brands in the market, the main aspect behind the Genero brand is to celebrate kids who maybe don't have a creative outlet to look at for inspiration. Comparing towards larger, corporate brands, Genero is meant to be loud and proud of messy doodles and outlandish characters.
