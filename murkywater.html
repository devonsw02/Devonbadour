MURKY WATER

PROJECT BRIEF

Conceptualize and design a business with consistent branding, products, and signage.

STRATEGY

Murky Water takes a local and obscurist approach to cafe branding, combining unique hand-drawn imagery with geometric patterns to create a distinctive brand.

IDENTITY

The visual identity combines a restrained modernist structure with expressive, hand-drawn elements. The system creates a balance between repetition, texture and unexpected visual moments.

THE STORE LOCATION

In the conception stage of the branding, Murky Water was idealized as an independent, local cafe in an urban setting. Somewhere to get late night work done, spend time with your friends, or simply relax.
